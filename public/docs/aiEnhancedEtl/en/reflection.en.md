## Reflection & Future Work

### Achievements
- Delivered a functional, end-to-end AI-driven ETL framework for multimedia data.
- Validated modular architecture capable of integrating diverse AI models.
- Achieved semantic search and retrieval across multiple modalities.

### Lessons Learned
- Early architectural planning (DAI pattern) simplified later scalability.
- YAML-driven configuration greatly improved flexibility for non-technical users.
- Model benchmarking was critical: choosing Clip4Clip over heavier video models balanced cost and accuracy.

### What Could Be Improved
- Centralized logging and monitoring for distributed containers.
- Unified UI for both ETL execution and search.
- Integration tests across different hardware environments.

### Next Steps
1. Add **image and document modalities** with OCR and vision models.  
2. Deploy **cloud-ready version** with autoscaling and monitoring.  
3. Extend the conversational assistant into a full **RAG-based orchestration agent**.  
4. Incorporate **real-time inference** for live video/audio streams.

---

**AI-Enhanced ETL** demonstrates how generative AI can transform traditional data pipelines into adaptive, multimodal systems — bridging the gap between raw content and actionable knowledge.
